Understanding Sea Level Rise

Global sea levels have been rising due to climate change, primarily caused by thermal expansion of warming ocean waters and melting ice sheets. This interactive visualization shows historical trends and projections.

Major Impacts of Rising Sea Levels

  • Coastal Flooding: Increased frequency and severity of coastal flooding affecting millions of people
  • Land Loss: Erosion and permanent inundation of low-lying coastal areas and islands
  • Ecosystem Disruption: Saltwater intrusion into freshwater systems and destruction of coastal habitats
  • Infrastructure Damage: Threat to coastal cities, ports, and critical infrastructure
  • Population Displacement: Climate migration as coastal communities become uninhabitable
